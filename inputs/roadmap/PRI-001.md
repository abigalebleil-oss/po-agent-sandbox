# PRI-001: Held results don't silently delay patient care

Source: synthetic roadmap item. All people, sites and numbers are fictional.

## Customer need statement
Lab staff and the clinicians who depend on them need to know when a patient result is held,
why it is held, and what to do about it, so that QC holds stop silently delaying patient
care without weakening the safety of the QC process.

## Who
- Night-shift medical laboratory scientist (see `inputs/feedback/01_night_shift_tech.md`)
- Core lab supervisor (see `inputs/feedback/02_lab_supervisor.md`)
- LIS / interface administrator (see `inputs/feedback/03_lis_admin.md`)

## Why now
QC-warning holds are one of the top reasons the lab misses its 60-minute ED potassium
turnaround-time KPI.

## Target release
Release 2027.1

## Linked requirements
REQ-001, REQ-002, REQ-003, REQ-004

## Non-negotiables
- A result held for a QC reject is never released by override or automation.
- Every manual release is attributable to a person and a reason.
- Only standard HL7 v2.5 segments are sent to the LIS.
